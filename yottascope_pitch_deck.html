The game

A million-dollar problem.

Pomegranate Corp offers you a simple deal: enter an abandoned school, collect corporate documents, and walk away with one million dollars.

The problem is that the school isn't really a treasure hunt. The entire challenge is designed around getting you inside.

The promise$1,000,000
01

The Offer

Explore the abandoned school, recover company documents, follow the rules, and claim your reward.

02

The Reality

The challenge is a trap. The reward is fake, the company is hiding the truth, and you are the real target.

Why EFS1?

The fear isn't just what's inside.

EFS1 combines corporate satire, environmental mystery, exploration, and player choice into a compact horror experience.

01

Corporate Horror

The company itself becomes part of the horror.

02

Trust Is Gameplay

Instructions may help you progress, but following them blindly can be dangerous.

03

The Pomephone Matters

A diegetic phone interface gives the player a direct connection to the outside world.

04

Multiple Outcomes

Your decisions determine whether the company's plan succeeds.

Gameplay

You decide what to believe.

The core experience is exploration, environmental storytelling, investigation, and making the right call when the evidence starts contradicting the company.

Bad Ending

Trust Pomegranate.

Follow the company's instructions, reach the final zone, accept the physical check, and drink the celebratory coffee. The company's plan succeeds.

True Ending

Call for help.

Use the Pomephone P1 Max E, contact emergency services using 911 or 112 depending on region, report the illegal activity, and escape the school alive.
