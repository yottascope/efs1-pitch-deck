World & story

The challenge is not what it looks like.

Pomegranate Corp. offers an unbelievable challenge: enter an abandoned school and complete the run for a chance at $1 million.

What begins as an unusual corporate promotion becomes a survival story as the player explores the school, discovers evidence hidden throughout the building, encounters black chemical sludge, and learns that Pomegranate's promises may have a very different meaning.

The school contains multiple floors and areas, each contributing to the mystery. Environmental storytelling and investigation are central to understanding the truth behind the challenge.

Floors -2 to 0

Underground and lower areas where the situation begins to become increasingly strange.

Floors 1 to 3

The main school areas, filled with clues, exploration opportunities, and escalating danger.

Floor 4

A deeper part of the mystery and one of the places where the player's choices matter.

Multiple outcomes

How the challenge ends is up to you.

EFS1 uses player choice to support multiple outcomes, including survival and failure.

Bad Ending

Trust Pomegranate.

The player accepts the company's story and follows its path, leading to the bad ending.

True Ending

Call for help.

The player calls emergency services, escapes the school, and reaches the true ending.

Dead Ending

Don't survive.

The player is caught by the sludge monsters and dies before escaping the school.

Why EFS1

A focused horror experience with a clear identity.

EFS1 combines corporate satire, environmental mystery, exploration, survival, and player choice into a compact horror experience.

Distinct concept

The central $1 million corporate challenge gives the game a recognizable hook while leaving room for mystery and dark humor.

Player-driven outcomes

Investigation and decisions matter, with multiple endings that encourage players to think about what they discover and who they trust.

Small-team development
