Escape from the School 1

A corporate horror game where a mysterious company lures you into an abandoned school with the promise of one million dollars.

Yottascope | Coming October 28, 2027

The Game

What They Tell You

  • Pomegranate Corp offers $1M
  • Explore an abandoned school
  • Corporate treasure hunt
  • Easy money opportunity

What's Really Happening

  • It's a trap
  • Coffee is poisoned
  • The check is fake
  • You're the real target

Gameplay

Bad Ending

  • Follow Pomegranate Corp orders
  • Drink the coffee
  • Accept the fake check
  • Game over

True Ending

  • Find the Pomephone P1 Max E
  • Make the 911 call
  • Call from school or Pomegranate HQ
  • Escape and survive

The World

Environment

A multi-level structure designed as a puzzle trap by Pomegranate Corp:

  • Floor -2: Secret vent access only
  • Floor -1: Flooded basement
  • Floor 0: Entrance / Main hub
  • Floors 1-3: Classrooms and exploration areas
  • Floor 4: Open air terrace

The Threat

An enclosed courtyard filled with black chemical sludge, which is a bioweapon engineered by Pomegranate Corp to eliminate evidence and witnesses.
